| D-015 | 2026-11-25 | Footprint conventions. Bars are aligned to UTC epoch multiples. The ladder includes untraded buckets inside the bar range as 0. | D-016 | 2026-11-25 | Profile conventions. Rows have size `tpo_tick`. POC = row midpoint, VAH = upper edge of the top row, VAL = lower edge of the bottom row. TPO counts 30-min periods per row from 1m high/low. HVNs are strict local maxima (plateau midpoint) of the moving-average-smoothed VP above `hvn_prominence`·max; an LVN is the minimum between adjacent HVNs. Single prints are not implemented (optional in the spec, added to BACKLOG). | Classic Steidlmayer value-area rule, documented and unit-tested, including ties and edges. |
